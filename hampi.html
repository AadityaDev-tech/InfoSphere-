Hampi

Overview

Hampi is a village and ruined city in Karnataka, on the Tungabhadra River. It was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire.

  • The site is spread over a landscape of granite boulders and hills.

History

The Vijayanagara Empire was founded in 1336 by Harihara I and Bukka Raya I, and its capital grew into one of the largest cities of its time. In 1565 the empire's army was defeated at the Battle of Talikota, and the city was then sacked.

Monuments

Key sites include the Virupaksha Temple, which is still in use, the Vittala Temple with its famous stone chariot, and the Lotus Mahal.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

The Group of Monuments at Hampi was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1986.
