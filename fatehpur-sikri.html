Fatehpur Sikri

Overview

Fatehpur Sikri is a city near Agra, Uttar Pradesh, built by Akbar as his capital. It is made largely of red sandstone.

  • It lies about 40 km west of Agra.

History

Akbar built it between 1571 and 1585 near the village of Sikri, home of the Sufi saint Salim Chishti. The name Fatehpur means city of victory, and the court left around 1585.

  • A shortage of water and Akbar's campaigns in the northwest are commonly suggested reasons for leaving.

Architecture

The city mixes Hindu, Jain and Islamic styles. Major buildings include the Jama Masjid, the Diwan-i-Khas, the Panch Mahal and the tomb of Salim Chishti.

  • The Buland Darwaza, the great gateway of the mosque, is about 54 m high and was built around 1601.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Fatehpur Sikri was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1986.
