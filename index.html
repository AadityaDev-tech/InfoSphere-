Forts

Battles

  • First Battle of Panipat1526: Babur defeats Ibrahim Lodi.
  • Battle of Haldighati1576: Maharana Pratap faces a Mughal army.
  • Battle of Plassey1757: the East India Company wins in Bengal.
  • Battle of Buxar1764: the Company defeats three allied powers.
