Agra Fort

Overview

Agra Fort is a red sandstone fort on the right bank of the Yamuna River in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. It was an important residence of the Mughal emperors.

  • The fort lies about 2.5 km from the Taj Mahal.

History

Akbar rebuilt an older fort on the site, with construction from 1565 to 1573. His grandson Shah Jahan later replaced many sandstone buildings with white marble ones.

  • Shah Jahan spent his last years confined in the fort after his son Aurangzeb took power. He died in 1666.

Architecture

The fort has a semicircular plan with high double walls and a moat. Notable buildings include the Jahangiri Mahal, the Moti Masjid, the Diwan-i-Am and the Musamman Burj.

Cultural Significance

Together with the Taj Mahal, the fort makes Agra one of the most visited heritage cities in India.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Agra Fort was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1983.
