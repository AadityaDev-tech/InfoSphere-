Battle of Plassey

Overview

The Battle of Plassey was fought on 23 June 1757 between the British East India Company, led by Robert Clive, and Siraj ud-Daulah, the Nawab of Bengal. The Company won.

  • It took place at Palashi, on the bank of the Bhagirathi River in Bengal.

Background

Tensions rose over the Company's fortification of Calcutta and its trade privileges. Siraj ud-Daulah captured Calcutta in June 1756, and Clive retook it in January 1757.

The Battle

The Nawab's army was much larger, but his commander Mir Jafar had secretly agreed to support the Company. Heavy rain also hampered the Nawab's gunpowder, and much of his army never joined the fight.

Aftermath

Mir Jafar was made Nawab and Siraj ud-Daulah was killed. The Company gained great influence in Bengal, and the battle is seen as an early step toward British rule in India.

  • Company control grew further after the Battle of Buxar in 1764.
