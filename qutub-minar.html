Qutub Minar

Overview

The Qutub Minar is a tall minaret in the Qutb complex at Mehrauli, Delhi, built of red sandstone and marble.

  • It stands about 72.5 m tall and has five storeys.

History

Qutb ud-Din Aibak, the first ruler of the Delhi Sultanate, began the tower around 1199. His successor Iltutmish added more storeys, and in 1368 Firoz Shah Tughlaq rebuilt the top after lightning damaged it.

Architecture

The tower is decorated with bands of inscriptions and carved balconies. A narrow spiral staircase of 379 steps runs inside, but it is closed to visitors for safety.

The Complex

The complex also includes the Quwwat-ul-Islam Mosque and the Iron Pillar, which dates to the Gupta period and is known for resisting rust.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

The Qutb Minar and its monuments were inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1993.
