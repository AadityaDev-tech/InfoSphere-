First Battle of Panipat

Overview

The First Battle of Panipat was fought on 21 April 1526 at Panipat, in present-day Haryana. Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi, the last sultan of the Delhi Sultanate.

  • The battle is seen as the start of Mughal rule in India.

Background

Babur, who ruled Kabul, advanced into northern India in the 1520s. Many nobles of Ibrahim Lodi were unhappy with his rule, which weakened his position.

The Battle

Ibrahim Lodi had the larger army, but Babur used gunpowder artillery, matchlock soldiers and a defensive line of carts. His cavalry then struck the enemy flanks and rear, a method known as tulughma.

  • Ibrahim Lodi was killed on the field.

Aftermath

Babur took Delhi and Agra soon afterwards and founded the Mughal Empire. He secured it by defeating Rana Sanga at the Battle of Khanwa in 1527.
