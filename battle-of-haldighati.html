Battle of Haldighati

Overview

The Battle of Haldighati was fought on 18 June 1576 between Maharana Pratap of Mewar and a Mughal army led by Man Singh I of Amer, sent by Akbar.

  • It took place in a narrow pass in the Aravalli hills, in present-day Rajasthan.

Background

Akbar had taken Chittorgarh in 1568 and wanted Mewar to accept Mughal authority. Pratap, who became Maharana in 1572, refused to submit.

The Battle

Pratap's force included Rajput soldiers and Bhil archers. The fighting lasted only a few hours. Tradition remembers his horse Chetak for carrying him from the field after being wounded.

  • Historians differ on the result: the Mughals held the field, but Pratap escaped and was not captured.

Cultural Significance

Pratap is honoured in Rajasthan as a symbol of resistance, and a memorial to Chetak stands at Haldighati.
