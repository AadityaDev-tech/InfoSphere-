Kumbhalgarh Fort

Overview

Kumbhalgarh is a Mewar fortress in the Aravalli Range of Rajasthan, known for its long perimeter wall.

  • The wall is about 36 km long, one of the longest continuous walls in the world.

History

Rana Kumbha built the present fort in the 15th century, and it was completed around 1458. It is also the birthplace of Maharana Pratap, born in 1540.

  • It served as a refuge for the Mewar royal family in times of danger.

Features

The fort stands on a ridge about 1,100 m above sea level. Inside the walls are palaces, step-wells and many Hindu and Jain temples.

  • The Badal Mahal (Palace of Clouds) is the palace at the top of the fort.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Kumbhalgarh is part of the Hill Forts of Rajasthan, inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2013.
