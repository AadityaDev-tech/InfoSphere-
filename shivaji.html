Shivaji

Overview

Shivaji Maharaj (1630-1680) was the founder of the Maratha state and was crowned Chhatrapati.

  • He was born at Shivneri Fort, near Junnar, to Shahaji and Jijabai.

Rise

Shivaji began by capturing hill forts in the Western Ghats, starting with Torna in 1646. In 1659 he defeated the Bijapur general Afzal Khan at Pratapgad.

  • In 1665 the Treaty of Purandar made him give up several forts to the Mughals. In 1666 he visited Agra and escaped from confinement there.

Military and Navy

He used fast-moving guerrilla tactics suited to the hills, known as ganimi kava, and built a navy to protect the Konkan coast.

  • Sindhudurg, a sea fort, was built in the 1660s.

Coronation

Shivaji was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad on 6 June 1674.

  • He died at Raigad on 3 April 1680.

Legacy

Shivaji is widely honoured in Maharashtra. In 2025 UNESCO inscribed Maratha Military Landscapes of India, a group of 12 forts linked to Maratha rule.
