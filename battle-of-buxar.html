Battle of Buxar

Overview

The Battle of Buxar was fought on 22 October 1764 at Buxar, in present-day Bihar. A Company army under Hector Munro defeated the combined forces of Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daula of Awadh and the Mughal emperor Shah Alam II.

  • It took place near the Ganges River.

Background

After Plassey, the Company placed Mir Qasim on the throne of Bengal in 1760. He later clashed with the Company over trade taxes and was removed, after which he fled to Awadh and joined Shuja-ud-Daula.

The Battle

The allied army was larger, but the Company's disciplined troops and artillery won after a hard fight.

Aftermath

In the Treaty of Allahabad (1765), Shah Alam II granted the Company the diwani, the right to collect revenue, of Bengal, Bihar and Orissa.

  • The treaty made the Company a ruling power in eastern India.
