Shah Jahan

Overview

Shah Jahan (1592-1666) was the fifth Mughal emperor and ruled from 1628 to 1658. He was born Khurram, a son of Jahangir.

  • He is best known as the builder of the Taj Mahal.

Reign

He came to the throne in 1628 after a succession struggle. His wife Mumtaz Mahal died in 1631.

Buildings

He built the Taj Mahal, the Red Fort and the Jama Masjid in Delhi, and the city of Shahjahanabad. The Peacock Throne was also made for his court.

Later Years

In 1657 he fell ill and his sons fought for the throne. Aurangzeb won, and in 1658 he confined his father in Agra Fort. Shah Jahan died there in 1666 and was buried beside Mumtaz Mahal in the Taj Mahal.
